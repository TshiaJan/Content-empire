# Privacy Policy for AI Content Empire

**Effective Date:** September 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **AI Content Empire** ("Application", "we", "us", or "our"). We are dedicated to transparent data stewardship and safeguarding user privacy across our digital media, content generation, and omnichannel campaign simulation suite.

## 2. On-Device Storage & Content Handling
- **Locally Stored Creative Data:** All draft scripts, campaign pipelines, template customizations, simulated revenue models, and affiliate projections are stored and processed locally on your device via standard browser storage (`localStorage` / `IndexedDB`).
- **No Mandatory User Accounts:** The Application operates without mandatory login, account creation, or email verification.
- **Intellectual Property Custody:** Any copy, media plans, and campaign outlines produced within the simulation remain entirely within your device's custody. We do not claim ownership, copy, or index your creative assets on remote servers.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Telemetry
- **No Cross-Platform Tracking:** We do not track personal activities across external apps or websites.
- **Diagnostics & Network Stability:** In standard distribution builds, anonymous crash diagnostics and non-identifiable telemetry (such as device OS version, error stack traces, and app stability metrics) may be processed via standard Google Play Services or Firebase to maintain app uptime and stability.
- **No Commercial Data Brokering:** We never sell, monetize, or license your campaign data or user metrics to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. User Controls & Data Purging
- **Data Erasure:** Because campaign pipelines and creator databases are saved locally, you maintain total control over your records. You may instantly wipe all saved data by clearing the Application's cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.

## 5. Security Safeguards
We implement industry-standard local application security protocols to protect on-device data. We recommend maintaining device-level security features, such as biometrics or passcodes.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Application is designed for content creators, marketing professionals, and digital media operators. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this policy. Revisions will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
